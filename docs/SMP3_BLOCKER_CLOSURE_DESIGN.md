# Bounded feedback and ordered recovery design

Input: frozen-final candidate; wire, cumulative ACK, sequence and HELLO unchanged.

Healthy credit uses bytes: estimated service B/s * (0.25s queue allowance + clean ACK feedback horizon). The feedback is elapsed since first actual write began, including serialization, transport, receiver ordering and peer ACK scheduling, not physical RTT. The base horizon uses a rolling minimum of unreplicated same-generation samples, plus max(configured ACK interval + 50ms, one-quarter sample) scheduling allowance. Only when both legs are normal does ordinary credit also cover the fresh bounded maximum-feedback EWMA that produced the measured ACK service; probation/restriction excludes this extension. Samples expire after 30s and the feedback observation is capped at the existing 2s. Unknown feedback starts at the previous 0.25s allowance. Per-leg byte credit is capped at min(16MiB, MaxInflightFrames*configured chunk, MaxReorderFrames*configured chunk); the existing global inflight token cap still applies. No allocation accompanies credit changes. A new whole frame can overshoot the byte credit by less than one configured chunk; the independent global frame-token limit remains strict. Slow/blackhole safety is not disabled.

Recovery retains a small count AND byte allowance, growing only after three uniquely confirmed records and enough newly confirmed actual bytes for the current stage, at most once per ACK. A cumulative ACK waiting behind an earlier record is not proof of the later leg being slow: record first-frontier time starts its independent blocker timer. A late clean record can establish reachability when confirmed within the bounded frontier proof horizon; copies, invalidated generations and records without a real write start cannot. Actual byte lengths, not chunk*count, account for reservations. Normal allocation is permitted only when byte ramp reaches healthy credit or the existing frame cap. Seed remains a probe hypothesis, subject to credit and safety limits, never claimed as measured capacity.

Frontier rescue uses max(200ms, bounded clean feedback horizon of the owner and alternate). Ordinary restricted tails retain their original attempt/work clock. Only recovering probes whose own and alternate observed feedback baselines are both below 200ms receive frontier exposure grace; measured high-delay probes start at actual first write. Margin added to a feedback envelope does not change this baseline classification. A truly bad frontier is still rescued; unique-copy classification is conservatively invalidated on rescue claim. No SACK or new wire ACK is introduced. A cumulative feedback sample cannot identify physical capacity or independently identify transport RTT. Per-session feedback never merges unrelated server clients by LegID/IP/name; existing host registry ownership remains unchanged.

Stalled activation requires blocked local demand or a genuinely transmitted unfinished tail without delivered progress for at least max(configured activation window, 500ms, configured retransmit timeout). The tail must contain at least two configured chunks or have been transmitted at least twice; a single queued idle record is insufficient. Idle and normal delivered small flows retain threshold policy. Existing bounded queue refusal is direct overload evidence. Local activate is once-only; notification intent persists and each attached generation is queued once by nonblocking maintenance. Queue acceptance is distinguished from write completion and peer receipt. No per-tick goroutine is created.

Lock order: engine worker/lifecycle and scheduler locks retain existing order; ledger and admission snapshots are taken sequentially, never by ledger-to-admission callback. Notification never waits for a network write or a full queue. Local producer ownership excludes unsent records from ordinary retry until first enqueue finishes. Close cancels all existing bounded maintenance and wait channels. Detailed regressions and raw results will determine whether this design closes the observed cases; no longer acceptance windows are introduced.


Implementation refinements from preserved failed short experiments:
- Restricted historical tails retain their original work clock; fresh frontier grace applies only to probation probes. The first variants confused these and regressed slow throughput.
- Feedback minima are refreshed by clean normal-session ACKs, expire after 30s, and are not trained by restricted/probation samples. A stale minimum is not a permanent RTT fact.
- Each normal session separately smooths its own unique ACK service over >=250ms and <=2s windows. Credit uses min(physical scheduling estimate, 1.25*local ACK service), with two-chunk floor and existing hard caps. This does not alter the shared physical provider. Normal service learns upward after join/leave; probation completion clears old low service knowledge.
- A cumulative batch can uniquely confirm a later record behind a clean, written predecessor owned by the other leg. This establishes reachability, not restored capacity. The proof trace records the batch/frontier distinction; duplicates, stale transport records and unwritten records stay rejected.
- Per-stage proof counters are capped and reset after one expansion; surplus from a cumulative batch cannot finance later stages. A single long local Write cannot veto uniquely delivered service: buffered TCP backpressure and scheduling stalls are not restored-capacity measurements. The earlier local-write veto produced a real Native recovery failure and has been removed; bounded probe timeout, byte/count ramp and unique-service exclusion remain active.
- A fixed 128-event trace ring is opt-in; it stores sequence, actual bytes, assignment, first write, frontier, ACK, duplication, generation and recovery origin. No payload, per-frame log or goroutine is added.


Actual Native counterexamples retained:
- Final2 normal-tail rescue clock incorrectly included frontier exposure; content/EOF/drain passed but slow fixed windows were ~2Mbps. Final3 restored the original ordinary attempt clock; the separate probation clock remains frontier-aware.
- Final3 later slow windows were 82.8/80.0Mbps while recovering briefly reopened. A single cumulative batch must not turn record counts into several large byte expansions. The revised ramp starts from the actual first-probe payload size, needs >=3 uniquely confirmed records AND the current byte allowance in newly confirmed bytes, advances at most once per ACK, and resets per-stage credits. Counts are capped at 3 and byte proof credit at the current allowance; no historic surplus finances later stages. Generation reprobe starts with one record of unknown size and learns its actual payload size at assignment. Existing synthetic count-only recovery fixtures now exercise actual ledger retirements with real payload bytes.
- No acceptance window, B floor or fault schedule was changed. These intermediate Native failures remain failures in their own evidence directories.


Final service distinction tested by valid ledger callback replay:
- Unique delivery opens bounded probation; returning to ordinary allocation additionally needs measured unique ACK service >=10% of this session's surviving alternate service (captured at probe start). This is a low-service exclusion floor, not a claim that physical capacity is fully restored. A saturated probation window with enough actual pending bytes relative to the peer's conservative feedback envelope and measured service below that floor returns to restriction. The first batch of each ACK-service window is discarded to avoid crediting old delivery before the measurement window. Measurements use >=250ms windows and reset after >2s gaps. Cold priors are not measured service. Unknown peer service remains explicitly unknown.
- During probation, the healthy direction drains extra queue allowance while retaining its observed feedback pipeline plus configured ACK interval and 50ms extra queue. Previously accepted records retain their exit paths. Protected maintenance samples at 50ms so a 200ms probe deadline is actually serviced; default/legacy maintenance remains unchanged. No rescue/proof acceptance or throughput window was enlarged.
- New additive transport diagnostics report record/byte probe limits, per-stage confirmed bytes, last probe useful ACK service, this session's normal ACK service, and conservative ACK feedback envelope. These are not physical RTT/capacity measurements; zero without a completed window is unknown. No wire fields or new protocol acknowledgements were introduced.
- A separate deterministic EOF test reproduced an existing last-carrier control-write hang. CLOSE now queues nonblocking, retries within a single bounded finalization loop, and uses the existing configured recovery timeout to end an unavailable notification with an error. Explicit cancellation still closes the loop immediately. Successful local queue/write is not proof of peer receipt; normal EOF is checked by actual payload/EOF process tests.


High-delay refinements from retained failed windows:
- The 400ms normal healthy pipeline must not use a collapsed physical estimate or HOL-confounded ACK service while the alternate is restricted/probationary. Its captured, previously delivered peer service is retained as a bounded pipeline hypothesis. It is not new physical capacity knowledge and does not change the shared estimator.
- A measured ACK feedback horizon already covers high-delay service. In that case the probe timer starts at actual first write/attempt, not that horizon plus a new frontier exposure grace. Low-delay cumulative ordering retains its explicit frontier case. This avoids allowing a 1Mbps serializer through a doubled 400ms grace.
- Successful probation graduation starts one normalized assigned-service epoch without changing cumulative counters. The intentionally limited probation allocation cannot become a historical deficit to repay as a one-leg catch-up burst. This occurs once at graduation, never on routine queue/budget fullness.
- The failed 400ms 82Mbps stability and ~148Mbps recovery windows remain FAIL. The final acceptance still compares with the original per-RTT baseline and original fixed windows.

### 2026-10-14 补充：阶段采样与实际 ACK 管线

`ordinaryAssigned` 从 restricted 开启新 probe 时，清理旧 normal 阶段的 `serviceRate/serviceAt/serviceWindow/serviceBytes`，保留本次捕获的同会话 alternate opening hypothesis。旧非零服务速率的陈旧时钟不能让 fresh opening budget 失效。确定性原代码 red3、修复 race30 保留。

normal 两腿均无 restriction/probation 时，普通字节预算覆盖新鲜 `feedbackMax` 的已有有界累计 ACK 等待管线，同时保留 quarter-second 服务量、采样速率裁剪与原硬上限。这里的反馈包含排队和顺序等待，不能称为物理 RTT；原 2 秒观测封顶、30 秒采样过期、16 MiB 及全局 frame/reorder 上限不变。任一腿处于恢复探测时，不使用这项 normal 扩展扩大健康队尾。没有改变 200 ms rescue、700 ms proof 或 5 秒 cooldown。
